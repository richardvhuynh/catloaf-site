CatLoaf: Cozy Cat Puzzle

Slide every cat onto a sunny spot. Cats slide until they hit a wall, so plan each move.

Help

How do I play?

Swipe to slide the cats. Each one keeps going until something stops it. Get every cat onto a sun patch to finish the level.

I'm stuck

Use a hint or undo. When you run out, you can watch a short ad to fill them back up. With Remove Ads they're free.

I bought Remove Ads but I still see ads

Tap Play, then Restore purchases at the bottom of the level screen. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

My progress is gone after reinstalling

Progress comes back from iCloud. Check that iCloud is on for your Apple Account, open the game while online, and give it a moment to sync.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?

Tap the gear on the start screen to change music, sound effects and vibration.
