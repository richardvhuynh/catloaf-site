CatLoaf Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

CatLoaf: Cozy Cat Puzzle ("CatLoaf", "the game") is made by Richard Huynh. This page explains what information the game uses and why.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email or location.
  • Your progress is saved on your device and in your own iCloud account.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to show them.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Your game progress

Levels beaten, coins, skins, settings and whether you bought Remove Ads are saved on your device. If iCloud is turned on, a copy is kept in your iCloud account so it comes back when you reinstall or change phones. This copy is stored by Apple under your Apple Account. We can't read it.

Ads

CatLoaf is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: an ad between some levels, and an optional ad you can choose to watch for more hints and undos. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's IP address, device type and settings, an advertising or app identifier, and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

The game does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so ads are not personalized using Apple's advertising identifier.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before ads load. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.

Buying Remove Ads stops all ads between levels.

Purchases

The Remove Ads purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We only learn that the purchase was made, so the game can turn ads off. Apple's handling of your data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location.
  • We don't use analytics services.
  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • The game doesn't use the camera or microphone.

Children

CatLoaf is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we'll help remove it.

Deleting your data

Deleting the game removes the progress saved on your device. To remove the iCloud copy, go to iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage (or Storage) and delete CatLoaf's data there. For data held by Google, see Google's privacy controls.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
